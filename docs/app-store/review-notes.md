# WinterWatch-Pro App Review Notes

Last updated: July 30, 2026

## Review Notes Template

WinterWatch-Pro is a business operations app for snow and ice service teams. It is used by internal staff and customer organizations to manage field crews, time tracking, work logs, accounts, equipment, and notifications.

### Why location access is required

The app uses location while the user is working so it can:

- record shift clock-in and clock-out location
- record service check-in and check-out at customer accounts
- help identify nearby accounts for faster field logging

Background location is limited to active shift tracking and operational accountability while a user is on shift.

### Why photo access is required

Users can attach photos to work logs for service verification and operational recordkeeping.

### Why push notifications are used

Push notifications are used for operational alerts such as shift events, overtime alerts, and organization notifications.

## Reviewer Test Path

1. Sign in with the provided review account.
2. Open the Dashboard.
3. Start a shift.
4. Select an account and begin a work log.
5. Attach a photo and complete the log.
6. Open Admin if the review account has manager access.
